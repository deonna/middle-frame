Privacy policy

Middle Frame, last updated November 3, 2026

Middle Frame collects no data. It has no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no servers.

What it does. On youtube.com pages, it changes each video thumbnail's image address to YouTube's own auto-generated frame for that video (for example i.ytimg.com/vi/VIDEO_ID/hq2.jpg). Your browser loads those images from YouTube's image server, the same server that serves the normal thumbnails. If you switch on Calm titles, it also rewrites ALL-CAPS words in video titles on the page you're viewing. Nothing leaves your browser.

What it stores. Two on/off settings (Middle frames, Calm titles), saved with Chrome's extension storage so they follow your Chrome profile.

Permissions. storage saves those two settings. The content script runs only on youtube.com, because that's the only site it changes.

Questions. Open an issue at github.com/deonna/middle-frame.
